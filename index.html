What We Offer

Our Services

01
Full Load Transportation (FTL)

Dedicated vehicles for large shipments, ensuring faster delivery and secure handling across India. Ideal for high-volume or time-sensitive consignments.

02
Part Load Transportation (LTL)

Cost-effective solutions for smaller shipments by sharing vehicle space. Pay only for what you use, without compromising on safety or reliability.

03
ODC – Over Dimensional Cargo

Specialised transportation for oversized and heavy cargo. We arrange all government permits, route planning, and escort vehicles for safe execution.

04
Project Cargo

End-to-end logistics for large industrial and project-based shipments including machinery and heavy equipment. Managed from origin to project site.

05
Containerised Services

Efficient transportation of 20 ft and 40 ft containers for domestic and export/import requirements. Suitable for a wide range of industries and cargo types.

Infrastructure

Vehicles We Use

We maintain access to a wide range of specialised vehicles through our strong vendor network, ensuring the right vehicle for every cargo requirement.

Vehicle Type 01
Flatbed Trailer (40 ft)

The most versatile trailer for industrial and commercial cargo. The open flat platform allows loading from the sides, rear, or by crane — making it ideal for heavy machinery, structural steel, construction panels, and large packaged goods.

Payload capacity: Up to 25–30 tonnes
Suitable for: Machinery, steel, construction material, industrial panels
Platform length: 40 feet
Vehicle Type 02
Semi Low-Bed Trailer

Designed for transporting tall or heavy equipment where height clearance is critical. The lowered deck reduces the overall transport height, making it possible to safely move industrial drums, reactors, transformers, and oversized cylindrical equipment on public roads.

Payload capacity: Up to 40–60 tonnes
Suitable for: Industrial drums, reactors, transformers, cylinders
Secured with heavy-duty chains and lashing
Vehicle Type 03
Trailer for ODC / Steel Structures

Heavy-duty trailers configured for over-dimensional cargo such as large steel trusses, bridge girders, pre-fabricated frames, and industrial structures. All movement is planned with government permits, route surveys, and pilot vehicles where required.

ODC permits arranged for all state routes
Suitable for: Steel trusses, girders, pre-fab frames, structural steel
Pilot/escort vehicle arranged when required
Vehicle Type 04
Heavy Flatbed – Industrial Structures

Robust flatbed trucks for the movement of heavy pre-fabricated or structural industrial components. Suitable for repetitive project cargo movement where consistent vehicle capacity and loading height compatibility is essential.

Suitable for: Pre-fab structures, heavy steel frames, industrial components
Deployed for project-based repeated movements
Available in multi-axle configurations
Vehicle Type 05
20 ft & 40 ft Container Trucks

Standard ISO container trucks used for domestic and import/export cargo movement. Available in 20 ft and 40 ft configurations to accommodate varying volumes. Ideal for FMCG goods, consumer products, and general cargo requiring enclosed transport.

Available in 20 ft and 40 ft sizes
Suitable for: FMCG, packaged goods, export/import cargo
Enclosed, weather-protected transport

20 ft / 40 ft Container Truck

Vehicle Type 06
Closed Body Truck (20 ft / 32 ft)

Fully enclosed trucks ideal for FMCG, food products, consumer goods and packaged items that require protection from weather and dust. Available in 20 ft and 32 ft body variants to match shipment volumes efficiently and cost-effectively.

Available in 20 ft and 32 ft body sizes
Suitable for: FMCG, food products, packaged consumer goods
Fully enclosed, weather-protected body

Closed Body 20 ft / 32 ft Truck
