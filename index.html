Who We Are

BUILT ON TRUST, DRIVEN BY SPEED

Dinesh Cargo is a premier logistics and transport company with over a decade of experience moving goods safely and efficiently across India. From small parcels to full truckloads — we handle it all.

We combine local expertise with a wide pan-India network to offer seamless, cost-effective cargo solutions for businesses of all sizes.

  • Pan-India road and freight network

  • Competitive and transparent pricing

What We Do

OUR SERVICES

01
Road Transport

Full truckload (FTL) and part truckload (PTL) services across all major highways. Door-to-door delivery with GPS-tracked vehicles.

02
Express Cargo

Time-critical shipments delivered with priority handling. Same-day and next-day express options for urgent consignments.

03
Warehousing

Secure, managed warehouse facilities for short and long-term storage needs. Inventory management and pick-and-pack solutions.

04
Supply Chain Management

End-to-end supply chain consulting and execution. We optimise your logistics from procurement to last-mile delivery.

Last-Mile Delivery
